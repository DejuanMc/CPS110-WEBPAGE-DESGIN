New Paltz, New York
A location for life

Big opportunities in the Hudson Valley.

SUNY New Paltz combines a broad liberal arts education with professional preparation in a welcoming and creative college-town environment.

The campus is surrounded by the landscapes, history, businesses, and cultural resources of the Hudson Valley, with New York City within reach.

  • Hands-on learning and close faculty mentorship
  • Programs spanning the arts, sciences, business, and education
  • A lively campus community with diverse student interests

Academic possibilities

Build an education around your ambitions.

Explore disciplines that encourage critical thinking, practical experience, collaboration, and creativity.

A

Arts and Design

Develop your creative practice through studio work, performance, design, digital media, and art history.

B

Business

Study management, accounting, finance, marketing, analytics, and entrepreneurship in a collaborative setting.

E

Education

Prepare to support learners and communities through teaching, leadership, counseling, and educational research.

H

Humanities

Examine language, history, philosophy, culture, communication, and the ideas that shape human experience.

S

Science and Engineering

Investigate the natural and technological world through laboratory work, research, computing, and engineering.

L

Social Sciences

Explore society and behavior through psychology, sociology, economics, political science, geography, and related fields.
